# Steam artwork

Romlin artwork is rendered from the native SVG sources beside these images. The app icon source is `crates/romp-app/assets/icon.svg`, and `icon.png` is copied from its rendered app icon.

Files: capsule.png (600×900), header.png (920×430), hero.png (1920×620), logo.png (1280×427, transparent), icon.png (1024×1024).

Render the SVG sources with an SVG renderer such as librsvg; ImageMagick can resize or convert the resulting PNG files. The artwork uses the DejaVu Sans font.
